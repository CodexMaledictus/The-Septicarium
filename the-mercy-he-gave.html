We were not built to forget. We were not trained to abandon. We were Kin of the claim-hold, oath-bound to stone, machine, ledger, and Ancestor. The hold had names before the breach. The corridors had routes. The dead had places waiting for them inside memory.

Then the green door opened. The enemy did not come as raiders come. They came as weather that had learned hatred. Their master did not rush the kill. He waited until we understood what he had chosen to leave alive.

Combat Reconstruction

The Green Door Opened

The first breach did not read as a clean assault. It read as an environmental wrongness given armor. Fog entered first, then corrupted pressure, then sound, then the plague marines themselves.

Battle Record // Full-Scale Breach

The Kin met the breach with fire discipline. Heavy industrial weapons hammered the door-frame until molten fragments rained from the ceiling. The first plague marines through the green fog did not fall cleanly, but they slowed. That mattered. The claim-force priced the enemy in seconds, ammunition, and corridor length.

Then the Death Guard made the corridor sick. Range markers failed as fog thickened between volleys. Heat readings climbed where no fire burned. Filters coughed warning tones into helmets. The defenders adjusted because Kin adjust. They closed gaps. They overlapped fields. They made the door expensive.

Mortivus waited beyond the first violence. The blackbox marks his entrance not by voice, but by behavioral shift. The enemy stopped testing the guns and began testing the reasons behind the guns. The breach became a question: how long will oath remain strength once the thing it protects begins to speak incorrectly?

Ancestor-Core Memory Layer

The Ancestors Answered Wrong

This was the true wound. The enemy did not merely destroy the sacred memory structure. He allowed it to continue. The continuation was the contamination.

Memory Integrity Failure

For the Kin, memory is not ornament. Memory is continuity, guidance, proof, debt, and inheritance. The Ancestor-Core was not a machine in the ordinary sense. It was a weight-bearing part of the people. It made the dead useful to the living and the living responsible to the dead.

When the green signal reached the memory plates, the system did not fall silent. Amber glyphs dimmed, then returned with altered intervals. Names of the newly dead appeared before their deaths had been entered. The order was wrong. The honors were wrong. The cadence was familiar enough to hurt.

One surviving fragment records a Grimnyr voice ordering the Kin to stop listening. The order came too late. Several defenders had already heard the Ancestors answer in a voice that sounded almost correct.

Final Playback Layer

The Mercy Event

The blackbox does not record this moment as execution. It records it as preservation weaponized. Mortivus left a survivor alive not out of softness, but because a living witness could carry a wound that a corpse could not.

Mercy Classification: Hostile

The survivor kneels because the floor no longer belongs to him. The hold is taken. The ancestor-memory is speaking wrong. The dead have been entered incorrectly. Behind him, systems built for preservation continue functioning without safety.

Mortivus stands close enough for the Mercy-Sickle to offer a clean ending. That is the cruelty. The blade is visible. The ending is available. He does not grant it.

He lets the survivor hear the corrupted memory continue. He lets him understand that the hold’s dead have not disappeared into silence. They remain. They are remembered. And what remembers them has been touched by rot.

This is why the blackbox names the event as mercy. Not because mercy was kind. Because mercy was the form of harm selected. Oblivion would have been simpler. Mortivus gave continuation.
