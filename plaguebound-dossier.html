PLAGUEBOUND DOSSIER

Names preserved until they become useful infections.

This datavault contains the internal classifications, command contradictions, plague-lineage records, oath fragments, ascension omens, and identity disputes of those bound to The Rotfather’s Vanguard.

The Plaguebound Dossier does not produce clean biography. Clean biography is for the living, the obedient, and the soon-forgotten. Here, a warrior is recorded by what clings to him: titles, symptoms, witnesses, wounds, contradictions, and the rot that refuses to leave his name.

DATAVAULT PURPOSE

INTERNAL RECORD

What the Dossier Is

The Plaguebound Dossier is the Septicarium’s registry of those who serve, infect, command, contradict, endure, and transform beneath the shadow of Mortivus Classarion.

It records plaguebound figures not by rank alone, but by devotional pressure: what they survived, what they spread, what they lost, what they became, and what the archive suspects they are becoming.

Principle I Rank is less reliable than symptom.
Principle II A title is only useful when it continues to rot.
Principle III Conflicting records may all be true in the warp’s shadow.

DATAVAULT LORE

CENSURED HISTORY

Why It Was Built

The first Plaguebound Dossier was not written by a scribe. It was assembled from failed orders, corrupted vox returns, stained armor manifests, confessions extracted from dying serfs, and the muttering of plague-censers aboard The Rotfather’s Mercy.

Mortivus permitted the archive to continue because the Dossier served a purpose beyond memory. It watched the host from within. It studied loyalty as a contagion. It tracked which warriors merely obeyed and which had begun to ripen toward something stranger.

In time, the datavault became less a roster and more an omen-engine. It does not simply ask who serves the Vanguard. It asks who among them is closest to being claimed, transformed, elevated, or erased.

CLASSIFICATION DISPUTED

Mortivus Classarion is recorded as a Plague Champion by the oldest surviving tally-stains, yet later records refuse agreement. Some name him a Lord of Contagion. Some place him among Blightlord elites. A few forbidden witness-fragments imply he has stood where only the Deathshroud should stand. The Septicarium confirms none of these accounts. It preserves them all.

Appearance in the Records

Mortivus appears most often where classification has already failed. His armor is described as command plate, terminator plate, corpse-shrine, rusted reliquary, and living wound depending on the witness. No single description has survived contact with him unaltered.

Command Method

He favors attrition, contamination, morale collapse, and ritualized pressure. Mortivus does not merely seek to defeat an enemy position. He seeks to teach it that its fall began before the first shot was fired.

Occult Tendency

Unlike crude champions who mistake decay for simple ruin, Mortivus studies rot as a metaphysical law. He reads infection as scripture, counts symptoms as omens, and treats every battlefield as a body waiting to confess.

Daemonhood Aspiration

The Dossier does not state that Mortivus will ascend. It states that many records behave as if his ascension has already begun. Several entries written before major engagements contain titles not yet earned, wounds not yet taken, and victories not yet fought.

SEPTICARIUM VERDICT

ENTRY STATUS

The Archive Refuses Closure

Mortivus Classarion is not filed as a finished subject. He is filed as a developing contamination. His name remains active, unstable, and watched by the Dossier.

The Septicarium recognizes him as Plague Champion, commander, omen-bearer, and aspirant, but denies all attempts to make the record clean. Clean records are Imperial habits. The Death Guard know better.

Threat Nature Spiritual corrosion before physical annihilation.
Archive Concern Identity instability may indicate favor, mutation, or pending ascension.
Final Entry Note Do not resolve the contradiction. Feed it.
