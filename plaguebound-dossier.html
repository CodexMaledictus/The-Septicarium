DATAVAULT PURPOSE

INTERNAL RECORD

What the Dossier Is

The Plaguebound Dossier is the Septicarium’s registry of those who serve, infect, command, contradict, endure, and transform beneath the shadow of Mortivus Classarion.

It records plaguebound figures not by rank alone, but by devotional pressure: what they survived, what they spread, what they lost, what they became, and what the archive suspects they are becoming.

Principle I Rank is less reliable than symptom.
Principle II A title is only useful when it continues to rot.
Principle III Conflicting records may all be true in the warp’s shadow.

DATAVAULT LORE

CENSURED HISTORY

Why It Was Built

The first Plaguebound Dossier was not written by a scribe. It was assembled from failed orders, corrupted vox returns, stained armor manifests, confessions extracted from dying serfs, and the muttering of plague-censers aboard The Rotfather’s Mercy.

Mortivus permitted the archive to continue because the Dossier served a purpose beyond memory. It watched the host from within. It studied loyalty as a contagion. It tracked which warriors merely obeyed and which had begun to ripen toward something stranger.

In time, the datavault became less a roster and more an omen-engine. It does not simply ask who serves the Vanguard. It asks who among them is closest to being claimed, transformed, elevated, or erased.
