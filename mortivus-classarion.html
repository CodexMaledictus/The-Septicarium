The Classarion Method

How He Makes War Explain Itself

Mortivus rarely begins with the killing blow. He prefers the enemy to understand why it has already lost.

Immediate breach is often vulgar. A fortress that still stands can be made to betray itself more thoroughly.

II

Corrupt What the Walls Mean

Sanctity, chain of command, oath, prayer, and discipline are turned into infection routes.

III

Spoil the Prayers

Resistance becomes ritual. Ritual becomes rigidity. Rigidity becomes a seam through which rot enters.

IV

Delay the Final Assault

He waits until the defenders still possess weapons but no longer possess the meaning that taught them how to hold them.

Kill When the Lesson Can Spread

The finest death is not merely terminal. It is instructional to those still alive enough to misunderstand it.
