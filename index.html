Where Mercy Is Counted in Rot

You stand within a plague-intelligence sanctum aboard The Rotfather’s Mercy, vessel of the plaguebound host known as The Rotfather’s Vanguard. This archive does not gather memory for scholarship. It gathers for corrosion, targeting, doctrine, prophecy, reliquary study, and the patient conversion of resistance into tally.

Maintained beneath the obscure authority of Mortivus Classarion, called by some Blessed by Nurgle, the Septicarium preserves warband record, battlefield decay, hostile pattern, plague-liturgical scripture, and sealed relic intelligence. Enter gently. The archive is known to observe those who observe it.

Intelligence Mandate

The Work of the Septicarium

The Septicarium exists to weaponize remembrance. It gathers what lesser minds would call intelligence and renders it into contagion. Here are catalogued the flaws of Imperial fortresses, the predictable prayers of shrine-worlds, the brittle pride of xenos elites, the doctrinal fractures of rival heresies, and the spoor-patterns through which despair may be guided into useful bloom.

The Death Guard were never made for haste. They were made for inevitability. Where other powers seek ecstasy, revelation, excess, or sudden ruin, the sons of Mortarion advance by patient certainty. Every wound is a gate. Every delay is a host. Every fortress is already sick, though it may not yet know the name of its illness.

Thus the Septicarium marks, measures, and remembers. What is remembered here does not remain inert. It ripens.
