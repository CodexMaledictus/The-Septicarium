INTELLIGENCE MANDATE

The Work of The Septicarium

DOCTRINAL

The Septicarium exists to weaponize remembrance. It gathers what lesser minds would call intelligence and renders it into contagion.

Here are inscribed the flaws of Imperial commanders, the predictable prayers of shrine-worlds, the brittle pride of Astartes, the adaptive hungers of xenos breeds, the doctrinal fractures of rival heretics, the spoor-patterns of retreat, and the liturgical openings through which despair may be introduced.

The XIV Legion was never made for haste. It was made for inevitability. Where other powers seek ecstasy, revelation, excess, or sudden ruin, the sons of Mortarion advance by patient certainty. Every wound is a gate. Every delay is a host. Every fortress is already sick, though it may not yet know the name of its illness.

Thus The Septicarium catalogs not merely enemies, but ripening conditions. It seeks where faith has thinned, where supply has faltered, where command has grown proud, where flesh has weakened, where morale has curdled, where ritual has become hollow, and where the smallest corruption can be entrusted to grow into a planetary catechism of rot.

What survives scrutiny is marked. What is marked is measured. What is measured is entered into the tally. What is entered into the tally is no longer separate from the mercy of Grandfather Nurgle.
