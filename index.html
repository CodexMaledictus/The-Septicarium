What Is The Septicarium?

A clean explanation for new readers, without draining the in-universe tone out of the page.

Archive Status: Breathing

The Septicarium is an in-universe Death Guard archive built around The Rotfather's Vanguard, a plague warband operating from the vessel known as The Rotfather's Mercy.

Every page is treated as a recovered file: a battle record, a relic examination, a command entry, a plaguebound dossier, or a corrupted scripture.

Some records are tactical. Some are devotional. Some should not have survived long enough to be read.

Scroll-Witness Format
I // The Vessel

The Rotfather's Mercy

The vessel does not sail. It advances like a wound learning geography. Decks breathe beneath plated rust. Bulkheads sweat old war. Somewhere below the command sanctum, engines pulse with the patience of an organ that has mistaken machinery for flesh.

II // The Archive

Memory as Contagion

The Septicarium does not preserve knowledge to keep it safe. It preserves knowledge so it can ripen. Every battlefield report, every relic sketch, every broken prayer and dying testimony is weighed for future usefulness.

III // The Warband

The Rotfather's Vanguard

The warband does not hurry toward conquest. It lets resistance exhaust itself. It lets hope ferment. By the time the Vanguard arrives in force, many worlds have already begun explaining their own surrender as wisdom.

IV // The Lie of Mercy

What the Survivors Say

Recovered witnesses often describe the same final confusion: that the pain lessened when they stopped resisting. The archive records this as doctrinal evidence. The living call it horror. The Septicarium calls it mercy becoming legible.

V // The Warning

Do Not Trust Clean Text

Clean records are the easiest to corrupt. A stain announces itself. A wound has honesty. But a perfect sentence can carry infection across a thousand minds before the first reader notices the fever.
