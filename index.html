INTELLIGENCE MANDATE

DOCTRINAL

The Work of The Septicarium

The Septicarium is the occult intelligence organ of The Rotfather’s Mercy.

It does not gather knowledge for preservation. It gathers knowledge so that resistance may be understood, weakened, infected, and returned to Grandfather Nurgle in a more useful state.

Within its datavaults are enemy doctrines, failed quarantines, plague omens, battlefield regressions, relic contaminations, devotional fractures, and the slow arithmetic of despair.

The XIV Legion was never made for haste. It was made for inevitability. Where lesser powers hunger for sudden revelation, the sons of Mortarion advance by attrition, endurance, and the patient certainty that all sealed things eventually open.

Here are inscribed the flaws of Imperial commanders, the predictable prayers of shrine-worlds, the brittle pride of Astartes, the adaptive hungers of xenos breeds, the doctrinal weaknesses of rival heretics, and the smallest spiritual fissures through which plague may be invited.

What survives scrutiny is marked. What is marked is measured. What is measured is entered into the tally. What is entered into the tally is no longer separate from the mercy of Grandfather Nurgle.
