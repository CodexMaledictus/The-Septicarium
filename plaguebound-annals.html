Where every war-form is named, chained, studied, and remembered as rot.

The Plaguebound Annals are the internal record-vault of The Rotfather's Vanguard. Here, commanders are not listed as entries. Infantry are not reduced to numbers. Engines are not catalogued as machines. Every formation is preserved as a living war-function inside the slow anatomy of the plague host.

The Annals exist to bind unit identity, battlefield doctrine, weapons, afflictions, relics, visual markings, and future chronicles into one diseased body of record.
