The Rotfather's Mercy // Lower Archive Nave // Annals Seal-Rite

The Plaguebound Annals

Where the warband is lowered into memory by chain, wound, oath, and rust.

The Plaguebound Annals are not read. They are descended into. Beneath the Command Deck of The Rotfather's Mercy, the sealed record-vaults breathe through brass vents, plague-censers, and the slow scrape of chains dragged across wet iron.

Here the host is preserved by what it has become. Commanders are pressed into subject-script. Infantry are counted by footfall and contagion. Engines are recorded by hunger. Relics are weighed by the wounds that still remember their touch. Battles are not summarized here. They are embalmed until the field speaks again.

No clean inventory survives the Annals. Every record must rot into doctrine before the vault accepts it.

Seal of the Annals // Rot-Index Rite

The Seal Does Not Open. It Judges.

The Annals turn on a diseased seal-wheel buried below the Command Deck. Each spoke answers to a different weight of memory: command presence, infantry procession, terminator oath, engine hunger, relic touch, battlefield witness, and the unborn records still chained in darkness.

Seal-Wheel Responsive
Muster-Vaults // Lower Archive Chambers

The Host Is Kept by What It Does to War.

The chambers below do not divide the warband for convenience. They divide it by plague-function. Flesh that advances is kept apart from engines that hunger. Relics are sealed away from the hands that wield them. Battle records are lowered into darker shelves until the field can be made to speak again.

Touch a Chamber to Wake It
Record-Cant // How the Annals Accept a War-Form

A Record Must Rot Before It Is Trusted.

No formation enters the Annals as a list. The vault demands identity, function, affliction, weapon, endurance, behavior, association, failure, and witness. Only then is the war-form considered stable enough to chain.

Record-Cant Active
War-Script // The First Opened Chambers

The Annals Are Built Like a Diseased Cathedral.

The archive chamber, command sanctum, infantry procession, and engine bay are not illustrations. They are organs of the same ship-body. Each one teaches the visitor how a different kind of plague memory is stored.

Chamber-Body Revealed
War-Chronicles // Battle Memory Table

A Battle Is Accepted Only When It Leaves Evidence.

The Annals do not crave every skirmish. They receive the wars that leave marks: broken bells, dead engines, enemy testimony, failed charges, impossible survivals, grudges, reversals, and wounds with enough memory to be catalogued without dying.
