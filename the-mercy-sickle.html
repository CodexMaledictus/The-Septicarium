The Mercy-Sickle

A spear-hafted plague sickle carried like a final argument.

The Mercy-Sickle is not a farming crescent, nor a berserker's sweeping scythe. It is a long execution-polearm: a spear-like haft bearing a shorter, thicker curved blade near the head.

Mortivus does not flourish with it. He advances, arrests resistance, and turns the chosen target into instruction for those still watching.

Expanded Doctrine Plate

Use and Mercy

The Mercy-Sickle appears in record less as a tool of haste than a terminal instrument of selection. Mortivus does not whirl it wildly. He introduces it once the chosen target has already begun to understand their own isolation. The weapon's reach therefore functions psychologically before it functions anatomically.

It harvests position, certainty, and witness-value before it harvests flesh. Commanders, priests, champions, elite survivors, and anyone whose breaking would teach nearby ranks are favored when Mortivus wishes a lesson to spread.

Expanded Perfumed Refusal Plate

Short Lore Record

The enemy came lacquered in perfume, silk-light blasphemy, and impossible speed. They shattered the first containment line, tore standard units apart, and moved through the corridor as though pain were choreography. One champion, bright with obscene confidence, reached Mortivus himself and opened a wound deep enough to survive the battle in memory.

What followed gave the weapon one of its strongest names. Mortivus gave ground without yielding shape. He let the champion enter certainty, turned the long haft, fixed the inner bite behind the enemy's forward motion, and drew the Mercy-Sickle in close. The thick curved blade did not kill the champion quickly. It removed the performance from them first. By the end of the exchange, the corridor no longer smelled like perfume. It smelled like instruction.
