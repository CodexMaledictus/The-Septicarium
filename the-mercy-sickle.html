The Mercy-Sickle

A spear-hafted plague sickle carried like a final argument.

The Mercy-Sickle is not a farming crescent, nor a berserker's sweeping scythe. It is a long execution-polearm: a spear-like haft bearing a shorter, thicker curved blade near the head.

Mortivus does not flourish with it. He advances, arrests resistance, and turns the chosen target into instruction for those still watching.

The Thick Curved Mercy Blade

This is the part that gives the weapon its name: not a thin farming sickle, but a short, heavy curved execution blade mounted to a spear-like haft.

Short Blade, Heavy Sentence

The blade is shorter and thicker than a common scythe or crescent weapon. It is a dense curved killing head fixed near the end of a long haft.

Use
Used at close-to-mid reach to catch armor, punish forward movement, and drag selected enemies out of formation.
Effect
The target feels the weapon's mass before the edge completes the wound.
Warning

Closing Assessment

It Ends Interpretation

The Mercy-Sickle remains under watch because it is more than a weapon and less than a relic in the comfortable sense. It is a disciplined execution-polearm whose shape makes doctrine visible.

The long haft extends selection. The short curved blade makes the end feel personal. The ring remembers. The tags apologize. The cut does not merely kill. It ends the target's final interpretation of what survival meant.
